Privacy policy

The game "Bag of Bags" (the game). Effective from 14 October 2026.

What the game stores

Progress, stars, coins, the collection, tasks and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own, and we do not receive this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex LLC). To show and count ads, the advertising SDK may process the device's advertising ID, device and operating system information, the IP address and interactions with ads. Yandex processes this data under its own policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect children's data; advertising data is processed as described above.

Contact

Questions about data: serzakharevich@yandex.by.
