City Zero

The end of the world has come to your city. Survive 7 days.

You wake up in your flat in Minsk, Moscow or Saint Petersburg. Sirens outside, panic in the building chat. The city you know by heart has turned deadly. What will you do?

  • Nine catastrophes: zombies, an AI uprising, an alien ship, a fog that swallows people, creatures from underground, cats taking over the planet, a city wrapped in silk overnight, a city that can't stop working, and the Children of the Flame cult.
  • Real cities: Minsk, Moscow and Saint Petersburg — real districts, metro stations, markets and bridges.
  • Choices have consequences: let the wounded neighbour in? Give away your last medkit? The game remembers everything.
  • Companions: Artyom the courier, Anya the medical student, Sergey Ivanovich, your neighbour Valentina Petrovna and Bublik the dog.
  • 81 endings — and one mystery that ties every catastrophe together. And a secret city.
  • Plays offline, in English and Russian.
