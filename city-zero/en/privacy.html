Privacy policy

The game "City Zero" (the game). Effective from 15 October 2026.

What the game stores

Progress, saves, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own, and we do not receive this data.

Advertising

The game may show ads from Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex LLC). In versions of the game without ads the advertising SDK is not started and processes nothing. To show and count ads, the advertising SDK may process the device's advertising ID, device and operating-system details, the IP address and data on interaction with ads. Yandex processes this data under its own policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics services.

Children

The game is intended for users aged 16 and over and does not knowingly collect children's data.

Emails to support

"Contact support" in Settings opens your mail app. If you send an email, we receive your email address and the text and use them only to reply to you.

Contact

Support and questions about data: serzakharevich@yandex.by.
