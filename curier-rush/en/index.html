Courier Rush

The food is getting cold — run!

You're a food delivery courier. Grab the order at the restaurant and reach the customer while the pizza is still hot and the soup hasn't spilled. Dodge pedestrians, wait for the green light or risk running the red, hop on a bus or a scooter — and collect your tips.

  • A real city: Moscow's residential blocks, Tverskaya, car-free Arbat, Moscow City and the riverside park.
  • Deliver on time: burgers, pizza, sushi, shawarma, dumplings, coffee — and soup that spills way too easily.
  • Rules of the road: traffic lights, crosswalks, drivers who won't yield, and traffic police.
  • Catch a ride: scooters, bikes, buses and taxis.
  • City crowds: grannies with shopping trolleys, dog walkers with long leashes, tourists and flyer promoters.
  • Three couriers: Pasha, Sveta and Alexander, each with their own story.
  • Play offline: on the subway, in a queue, anywhere.
