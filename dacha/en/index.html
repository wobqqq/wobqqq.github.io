My Dacha: Farm Story

An overgrown plot, a leaning little house… and six acres of possibility.

Clear the land, plant your beds, meet the neighbors and turn the plot into a dream cottage: a greenhouse, a gazebo, a samovar and a cat on the porch.

  • Plant, harvest, sell: radishes, cucumbers, tomatoes, strawberries, apple trees and two dozen more.
  • Your cottage grows while you're away: come back for the harvest and the takings of the roadside stall.
  • Neighbor orders: Granny Nina, Uncle Misha, Aunt Lyuba and Sergei from the market always need something.
  • Beetles, frosts and rain: a lively farm, no harsh punishments.
  • Your own house: a gazebo, a samovar, a hammock, hens, a cellar full of preserves.
  • Four seasons and holidays.
  • Play offline, in English and Russian. No purchases.
