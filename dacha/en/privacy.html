Privacy policy

The game "My Dacha: Farm Story" (the game). Effective from 16 October 2026.

What the game stores

Your plot, harvest, carrots and cherries, buildings, orders, tasks, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own, and we do not receive this data. The game does not need the internet.

Ads

The current version of the game has no ads. If an update adds them, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Analytics

The game does not use third-party analytics services.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Rating the game

The in-game rating dialog is shown by the RuStore app under its own rules; the game does not receive your data from RuStore.

Writing to the developer

The "Write to the developer" button opens your mail app with a prepared letter (game version, Android version, phone model). You see the letter before sending it; nothing is sent automatically.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect children's data.

Contact

Questions about data: serzakharevich@yandex.by.
