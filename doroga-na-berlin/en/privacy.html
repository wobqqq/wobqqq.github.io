Road to Berlin privacy policy

What data

The game does not collect personal data, store it on servers or pass it to third parties. It has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it does not need the internet. Right now the game has no ads and no analytics: the Yandex ads SDK is built in but switched off and never started (see "Ads"), and analytics and crash reports are off too (see "Analytics and crashes"). So the game does not go online at the moment.

What is stored on the device

Campaign progress, trophies, bought weapons, checkpoints and settings are kept only on your device so you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

How long and how to delete

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Road to Berlin → Storage → Clear data).

Ads

Both versions of the game (RuStore and AppGallery) contain the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (Yandex Advertising Network, Yandex LLC) with the VK Ads adapter (myTarget, VK LLC), through which Yandex may bring in VK Ads for some impressions. Ads are switched off now: the game does not start these SDKs, they do not contact any server and send nothing. The internet, network state and advertising ID permissions are in the game only because of this SDK.

If we switch ads on in an update, this policy will change before that update comes out. Ads will then appear only outside a fight: a video you choose to watch for a reward (such as "Second wind" after dying), between missions, and a banner in the dugout and the shop. The game is rated 16+, so ads may be personalised: to show and count ads the SDK will receive the device's advertising ID, device model, Android version, language, IP address and data about impressions and taps. You can turn personalisation off in the phone settings (reset or delete the advertising ID). The ad networks process this data under their own policies: Yandex — https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ (SDK terms: https://yandex.ru/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement/), VK — https://help.mail.ru/legal/terms/adsvk/privacy/. The game does not ask for your location and does not pass it to ad networks. The game itself does not store this data.

Analytics and crashes

The MyTracker analytics and Tracer crash-report services (VK LLC) are off in this version: the game has no keys for them and does not start them. No analytics or crash reports are sent. If we switch them on, this policy will change before such an update comes out.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above) and write to us with a question about data or a request to delete our correspondence. The game works fully offline: if your phone allows it, you can block its network access (Settings → Apps → Road to Berlin → Data usage).

Children

The game is rated 16+ and is not meant for children; it does not collect children's data.
