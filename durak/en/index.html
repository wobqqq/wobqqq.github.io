Durak

a card game with characters

Throw-in and transfer Durak, just like old times — but with company you'll want to beat.

Real characters sit at your table, each with their own style and jokes. Deal the cards — who's the fool today?

What is inside

  • Classic rules: throw-in and transfer Durak, 24- or 36-card decks, 2–4 players.
  • Opponents with personality: sly Granny Zoya, grumpy Grandpa Mikhalych, excitable Aunt Luda and cheeky student Artyom.
  • Courtyard championship: from newcomer to the "grandpa of the yard".
  • Hints and game review: find out where you could have played better.
  • Beautiful tables and decks: kitchen, veranda, train compartment, courtyard.
  • Plays offline, in Russian and English.
