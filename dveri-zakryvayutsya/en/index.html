Mind the Doors!

The train is pulling into the station: get the passengers off in time!

A metro car is a grid of passengers. The ones getting off wear a green arrow. Tap a passenger, then a free spot, and they walk there. Line them up by the doors or in a queue behind each other, and the doors open as they hop out onto the platform.

  • 300 stations on 20 metro lines: the bakery line, the garden line, the winter line, the seaside line…
  • Familiar fellow travellers: grannies in headscarves, a grandpa in a flat cap, a fisherman with his rod.
  • Something new on every line: handrails, trolleys, dozing passengers, "give up your seat".
  • No rush: no timer, and if you run out of moves, the driver will wait.
  • Train of the Day, daily tasks, hints and undo.
  • Plays offline, in English and Russian.
