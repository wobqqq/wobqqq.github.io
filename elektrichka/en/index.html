Train Seller

"Ladies and gentlemen, may I have your attention, please…"

You're a seller on a commuter train. Buy goods at the market, give a pitch the whole carriage can't resist, sell to everyone before the next stop — and slip away before the ticket inspectors arrive!

  • Build your pitch: "Works even in the frost!", "Buy one, get one free!", "Just like grandma's!" — gardeners, students, anglers and pensioners all want different things.
  • Keep your voice: shout over the rattle of the wheels, bridges and tunnels.
  • Sell everything: flashlights, socks, crosswords, glue that sticks anything, ice cream in summer and gloves in winter.
  • Watch out for inspectors! Move to the next carriage, hide in the vestibule — or just buy a ticket.
  • Grow your business: bigger bags, a trolley, charisma and new routes, from the country line to the airport express.
  • Reputation matters: honest sellers get loyal customers.
  • Play offline.
