Privacy policy

The game "Train Seller: Commuter Simulator" (the game).

What the game stores

Progress, settings, tasks and achievements are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own; we never receive this data.

Ads

The game shows ads from Yandex Mobile Ads. To show and count ads, the ad SDK may process the device's advertising ID, device and operating system details, the IP address and interactions with ads. Yandex processes this data under its policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings.

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Contact

Questions about data: support@trainseller.example.
