Epoch of Banners

A battery fires canister point-blank into a French column, a square holds off the cuirassiers to the beat of drums.

Command the Russian army in the Patriotic War of 1812 and the foreign campaigns of 1813–1814. Form your battalions in column to march, in line to fire and in square against cavalry, bring the batteries up to canister range and choose the moment for the bayonet charge.

  • The whole campaign from the Niemen to Paris: Mir, Smolensk, Borodino, the Berezina, Leipzig, Montmartre — 52 historical battles.
  • Honest history: until Borodino the army retreats; a battle's task is to delay the enemy and keep the army whole.
  • Formations and tactics of the age: column, line, square, volley and reload, canister, cavalry charges.
  • Every arm of both armies, veteran regiments, ranks and orders.
  • Plays offline, in Russian and English.
