Smile Factory

Build the perfect smile from puzzle teeth!

An open mouth waits in front of you, and the factory belt carries puzzle teeth. Drag them into the gaps and tap to turn them: a bump goes into a dent, upper and lower teeth close cusp to cusp. When everything fits, the jaws click shut and the customer flashes a big smile!

  • A logic puzzle of its own: think which tooth goes where.
  • 300 levels and 20 funny customers: a granny, a pirate, a singer, a cat and more.
  • Something new as you go: braces, crooked smiles, an extra tooth, a gold crown.
  • The smile album, smile of the day and daily tasks.
  • No rush, no losing: hints and undo are always at hand.
  • Plays offline, in Russian and English.
