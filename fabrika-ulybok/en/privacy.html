Privacy policy of Smile Factory

What data

The game does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts or other personal data; it has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. The game itself does not need the internet: without it, it works as usual. When the internet is on, only the third-party SDKs built into the game go online, and only with what is described below: in both versions, anonymous statistics through MyTracker and crash reports through Tracer (see "Statistics and crashes"); in the RuStore version, the RuStore rating SDK (see "Rating the game").

What stays on your device

Progress, settings and rewards are stored only on your device so you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

Reminders

Only if you agree, the game asks the phone for permission to show notifications and sets a local reminder: only after 7 days without opening the game, at most 3 times (after 7, 14 and 21 days), never at night. The phone shows it on schedule by itself, without the internet or any server; no data is sent anywhere. The permission to run after a restart is used only to set the reminder again. You can turn it off in the game's or the phone's settings at any time. Whether you turned reminders on and opened the game from one is counted in the anonymous statistics (see "Statistics and crashes").

How long and how to delete

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Smile Factory → Storage → Clear data).

Rating the game

In the RuStore version, the "Rate the game" button and an occasional offer to rate the game open RuStore's official rating dialog. It is shown by the RuStore rating SDK (VK LLC) built into the game; if the dialog is unavailable, the button opens the game's RuStore page instead. You leave your rating and review in the RuStore app under your RuStore account, and RuStore handles them under its own terms: https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users.

Each time the RuStore version starts, and if the rating dialog fails to open, the SDK sends technical metrics to a RuStore server (stats-dg.rustore.ru): the game's package name and version, the SDK's name and version, the event type and its time, error details if something failed, and a random installation ID that the SDK creates on your device. This ID is not linked to your phone model, your account or your advertising ID, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address. Without internet, the metrics wait on the device and are sent later by a daily background task, including after the phone restarts. That is why the RuStore version needs the permissions to access the internet, check the network state and run after a restart. These metrics contain no name, phone number, email, advertising ID, phone model, location or game progress. RuStore keeps them as its terms say.

The AppGallery version has no RuStore SDK: "Rate the game" opens the game's AppGallery page. The game does not receive your data from the store.

Statistics and crashes

To make the game better, it sends anonymous statistics through MyTracker (VK LLC) in its child mode: launches and play time, levels and tutorial progress, hints and undos used, reminders and in-game currency, plus technical details: phone model, system version, language, time zone and game version. If the game closes with an error, freezes or hits an error, a technical description of the failure (phone model and system version, game version, the place in the code, the last level opened) goes to Tracer (RuStore, VK LLC). There is no name, phone number, email, advertising ID, location, sensor readings or phone content in it; as with any connection, the server sees the IP address. These SDKs keep their own random installation ID on the device; it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Without internet, the data waits on the device and is sent later. VK processes the data under its rules: https://tracker.my.com/privacy, https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users, and keeps it as they say.

Writing to the developer

"Write to the developer" opens your mail app; you send the email yourself. Your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above); this also removes the statistics waiting on the device and the SDKs' installation IDs. To stop the game from sending anything (statistics, crash reports), block its internet access in the phone settings if your phone allows it (Settings → Apps → Smile Factory → Data usage): the game works fully without the internet. There is no separate statistics switch in the game. For data VK received, contact VK under its rules (links above). You can also write to us with any question about data or to have your emails deleted.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages (0+). It does not ask your age and does not receive your name, email, phone number, location or advertising ID. MyTracker statistics run in child mode.
