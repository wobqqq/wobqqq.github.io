Florist

Turn the stems and watch the bouquet bloom!

A bright little flower shop where every bouquet is made one tap at a time. Turn the stems until each flower reaches the ribbon: the buds open and the finished bouquet wraps itself in kraft paper with a bow.

  • Simple rules: one tap, one turn.
  • 12 flowers and customer orders: a birthday, a date, Mom, a wedding.
  • The florist's portfolio: 40 bouquets with recipes.
  • A bouquet of the day, tasks and holidays: Valentine's Day, Women's Day, Back to School, New Year.
  • Hints and undo: you never get stuck.
  • Play offline, in English and Russian.
