Just One Question

"Excuse me, just one question!"
"I was here first!"
"Sorry, we're on lunch break!"

You run the waiting room. Hand out tickets, send people to the right windows, deal with line-cutters and keep the crowd calm before chaos breaks out.

  • You know these people: the "just one question" guy, the granny who was definitely here first, the person with a mountain of paperwork and the loud phone call.
  • Post office, clinic, service center: every place has its own rules and surprises.
  • Check the paperwork: ID, yes — referral, no? Next!
  • Events: lunch breaks, jammed printers, no forms left and a surprise inspection.
  • Upgrade your hall: a water cooler, air conditioning, an electronic queue and cozy plants.
  • Quick 2–3 minute levels. Play offline.
