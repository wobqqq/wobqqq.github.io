Classroom 13

A night in an old Soviet school: hide in lockers, hold your breath and find classroom 13.

You stayed behind on duty and woke up at night. The board still says "April 24", the doors are locked, and the corridors are walked by those long gone. You cannot fight, only hide, shine your torch while the battery lasts and hold your breath when the steps stop at the locker door.

  • 7 chapters and 42 locations: corridors, the gym, the canteen, the workshop, the library, the basement and classroom 13.
  • The school's ghosts: the cleaner with her bucket, the PE teacher with his whistle, the labour teacher at his lathe, the deputy head with her own lamp.
  • Light and sound: the torch beam, moonlight, steps heard left or right (play with headphones).
  • Breathing: tap in rhythm while a ghost stands by your hide.
  • Notes about that night, the school museum and a hidden ending.
  • Works offline, in English and Russian; free checkpoints.
