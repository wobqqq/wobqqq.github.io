Cosmo Tentacles

Shoot a tentacle, grab on with a juicy "chpok", swing over a planet and fly through space!

You are Octo, a living glowing creature from deep space. The Iron Swarm has taken the galaxy. Grab rocks with a tentacle of the same colour, swing like on vines, hurl boulders at Swarm stations, rip out reactor generators and watch the planets light up again.

  • Physics you want to touch: inertia, planet gravity, swings, throws and destruction into shards.
  • Colour to colour: the red tentacle grabs red, the blue one grabs blue; three more colours open on the way.
  • Ten sectors of space: an asteroid belt, a giant's rings, a nebula, a black hole, a pulsar and the heart of the Swarm.
  • The Hangar: Octo's upgrades and twelve tentacle kinds: jellyfish, electric, ice, star, royal and more.
  • Play offline, in English and Russian.
