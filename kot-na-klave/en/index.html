Cat on Keyboard

A fluffy cat fell asleep on your keyboard and is typing nonsense to your boss. Move it away without waking it!

Slowly, paw by paw, fold its paws, head and tail, then carry the sleepyhead to a soft cushion. Watch the cat's eye: a quick move or the belly, and it wakes with a loud "Meow!".

  • 300 levels in 12 places: kitchen, sofa, office, café, train, server room, space.
  • 40 cat coats, from tiny kittens to a giant Cat CEO.
  • Dozens of keyboards: backlit mechanical ones, laptops, a synthesizer, a typewriter.
  • Cat of the day, tasks and an album of every cat.
  • No timer, plays offline, in English and Russian.
