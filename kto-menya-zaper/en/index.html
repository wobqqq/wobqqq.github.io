Who Blocked Me?

You're running late. And someone blocked your car in. Again!

Get every car out of the lot in the right order: one tap and a car drives off. But if someone is in the way — bang! — and the alarm goes off.

  • Easy to start, hard to stop: 30-second levels, one-finger controls.
  • 300+ levels: a courtyard, a mall car park, a market, snowy winter, the cottage.
  • Familiar moments: a "call me, I'll move" note, a cat on the bonnet, a barrier, a granny with a trolley.
  • Your own garage: 60 funny cars and a collection of windscreen notes.
  • A daily jam, tasks and achievements.
  • Plays offline, in Russian and English.
