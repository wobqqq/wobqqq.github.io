Privacy policy

The game "Who Blocked Me?" (the game). Last updated: 17 October 2026.

What data

The game does not collect personal data, store it on servers or share it with third parties. It has no accounts, ads, analytics of its own or in-app purchases, and it does not need the internet. The only part that goes online is the RuStore rating SDK, which is built only into the RuStore version (see "Rating the game"). The AppGallery version has no third-party SDKs, does not go online and collects or sends nothing.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, garage, notes, quests and settings are stored only on your device so you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

Reminders

Only if you agree, the game asks the phone for permission to show notifications and sets a local reminder: only after 7 days without opening the game, at most 3 times (after 7, 14 and 21 days), never at night. The phone shows it on schedule by itself, without the internet or any server; no data is sent anywhere. The permission to run after a restart is used only to set the reminder again. You can turn it off in the game's or the phone's settings at any time.

How long and how to delete

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Who Blocked Me? → Storage → Clear data).

Sharing

The "Share" button draws a picture of your car and the number of cars you have freed on the device and opens the system share sheet; you choose where and to whom to send it. The game itself sends nothing.

Rating the game

In the RuStore version, the "Rate the game" button and an occasional offer to rate the game open RuStore's official rating dialog. It is shown by the RuStore rating SDK (VK LLC) built into the game; if the dialog is unavailable, the game's RuStore page opens instead. You leave your rating and review in the RuStore app under your RuStore account, and RuStore handles them under its own terms: https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users.

Each time the RuStore version starts, and if the rating dialog fails to open, the SDK sends technical metrics to a RuStore server (stats-dg.rustore.ru): the game's package name and version, the SDK's name and version, the event type and its time, error details if something failed, and a random installation ID that the SDK creates on your device. This ID is not linked to your phone model, your account or your advertising ID, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address. Without internet, the metrics wait on the device and are sent later by a daily background task, including after the phone restarts. That is why the RuStore version needs the permissions to access the internet, check the network state and run after a restart. These metrics contain no name, phone number, email, advertising ID, phone model, location or game progress. RuStore keeps them as its terms say.

The AppGallery version does not contain the RuStore rating SDK at all: "Rate the game" opens the game's AppGallery page. That version does not go online, asks for no network permissions and sends no metrics. The game does not receive your data from the store.

Writing to the developer

The "Write to the developer" button opens your mail app with a prepared letter (game version, Android version, phone model, store); you send the email yourself, nothing is sent automatically. Your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above) and write to us with any question about data or to have your emails deleted.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect children's data.
