Privacy policy of Courier Rush: Delivery Runner

What data

The game does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts or other personal data; it has no accounts and no in-app purchases. The game shows ads, and they may be personalised (see "Ads"). The game itself does not need the internet: without it, it works as usual. When the internet is on, only the third-party SDKs built into the game go online, and only with what is described below, the same in both versions (RuStore and AppGallery): Yandex and VK ads (see "Ads"), anonymous statistics through MyTracker and crash reports through Tracer (see "Statistics and crashes"). The game itself downloads and sends nothing.

What is stored on the device

Progress, settings and rewards are stored only on your device so that you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

How long it is kept and how to delete it

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Courier Rush: Delivery Runner → Storage → Clear data).

Ads

The game shows ads through the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, YANDEX LLC), which fills some of them with VK Ads (myTarget, VK LLC): rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one (for example, to save an order or double your tips), an interstitial on the results screen after every third finished run or route (never during a run or in the tutorial), and a banner in the menu, on the district map and in the wardrobe.

The game is for players aged 6 and over and is not a children's game, so its ads may be personalised: the SDK does not run in its child-audience mode, and the game has the permission for the device's advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). To show, choose and count ads, the SDKs may receive the device model, Android version, language, IP address, the advertising ID and ad impression and click data. The AppMetrica analytics built into the Yandex SDK is turned off. The ad networks process this data under their policies: Yandex — https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ (SDK terms: https://yandex.ru/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement/), VK — https://help.mail.ru/legal/terms/adsvk/privacy/. The game itself does not store this data. The networks keep it as their policies say.

You can opt out of personalised ads or reset the advertising ID in the phone settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Google → Ads"); the ads are then non-personalised. Without internet no ads are shown and the game works as usual.

Statistics and crashes

To make the game better, it sends anonymous statistics through MyTracker (VK LLC): launches and play time, starts, wins, losses and restarts of runs and routes, ad offers and ads watched, which version of the game it is (RuStore or AppGallery), and technical details: phone model, system version, language, time zone and game version. Location, network and device-environment collection and sensor readings are turned off in MyTracker. As the game is not a children's game (6+), MyTracker does not run in its child mode and may receive the advertising ID; VK Ads may use MyTracker data to count ad impressions and clicks.

If the game crashes or freezes, a technical crash report (phone model and system version, game version, the place of the error in the code) is sent to Tracer (RuStore, VK LLC). Statistics and crash reports contain no name, phone number, email, location or phone content; as with any connection, the server sees the IP address. These SDKs keep their own random installation ID on the device; it is deleted with the game or when its data is cleared. While there is no internet, the data waits on the device and is sent later. Everything is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS) and is never sold. The data is processed under VK's rules: https://tracker.my.com/privacy, https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users. It is kept as those rules say.

Permissions

In both versions the game needs only three permissions: internet access and network state (for ads, statistics and crash reports; the game itself works offline) and the advertising ID (for ads, see above). The game sends no notifications and does not ask for that permission.

The studio's other games

The game menu has a list of the other games of Cat & Duck Studio. The list is built into the game: the game downloads nothing for it and sends nothing anywhere. Tapping a game in the list opens its page in the store.

Writing to the developer

The "Contact the developer" button in the settings opens your email app with the address and the subject filled in; you send the email yourself, nothing is sent automatically. Your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above): this also deletes the statistics waiting on the device and the SDKs' installation IDs. To stop the game from sending anything (statistics, crash reports, ad requests), deny it internet access in the phone settings, if your phone allows it (Settings → Apps → Courier Rush: Delivery Runner → Data usage): the game works fully offline. There is no separate statistics switch in the game. For data Yandex or VK received, contact them under their rules (links above). You can also write to us with a question about your data or to have our correspondence deleted.

Children

The game is for players aged 6 and over (6+) and is not made specifically for children: its ads may be personalised, and the ad and statistics SDKs do not run in their child mode. The game does not ask for your age and does not receive a name, email, phone number or location. Parents can deny the game internet access (see "Your rights") or turn off ad personalisation in the phone settings.

Changes

If the game starts to collect or share other data, this policy will change before that update is out.
