Panel House Lift

Ding! Take the neighbours to their floors and don’t overload the old lift.

You run the lift of a Soviet panel block. Tap a floor and the cabin goes there; tap a neighbour and they step in, then step out at their floor by themselves. Watch the weight needle: the red “Overload” lamp lights up and the lift won’t budge. Deliver everyone before the clock runs out, before Granny Zina starts grumbling!

  • 300 levels: from a five-storey block to a 16-floor tower, from one entrance to four.
  • 15 neighbours with their own ways: Granny Zina with seedlings, a bodybuilder with dumbbells, a mum with a pram, piano movers.
  • Rush hours and breakdowns: stuck doors, a flickering lamp, a sticking button.
  • Renovate the entrance: paint the walls, hang a mirror, plant flowers.
  • Neighbours album, achievements, shift of the day, tasks and holidays.
  • Pause and a 10-second rewind when you fall behind.
  • Works offline, in Russian and English, 0+.
