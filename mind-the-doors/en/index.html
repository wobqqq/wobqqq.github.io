Mind the Doors

Mind the doors! And this is your stop…

A subway car at rush hour. Squeeze in, grab a free seat and get off at your station before the doors close. "Excuse me!", "Are you getting off?" — every ride is a quick little puzzle.

  • Real rush hour: a guy with a bike, a tourist with a huge suitcase, a granny with a trolley and everyone glued to their phones.
  • Quick levels: 30–90 seconds, instant restart.
  • Politeness wins: let people off first, take off your backpack, give up your seat — and earn three stars.
  • Seven Moscow lines: 210 station levels, deep escalators and long transfers.
  • Collect all your fellow passengers in a funny album.
  • Daily ride: a new level every day.
  • Play offline — even underground.
