Privacy policy

Mind the Doors («Осторожно, двери закрываются!») does not collect or store your personal data. The game has no server and no accounts.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stops cleared, coins, wardrobe, settings) is kept only on your device and is deleted with the game.

Ads

The game shows ads from the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK). To show and count ads the SDK may use the device's advertising identifier and technical data (model, OS version, language). You can reset the advertising identifier and turn off personalisation in your phone's settings. See Yandex's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no data about children. The ad SDK is set to non-personalised ads where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked through the game's page in the app store (reviews or the developer contact section).
