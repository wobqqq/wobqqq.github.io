90s Rewind

Remember how the tape deck chewed your favourite cassette and you saved it with a pencil?

Untangle the tape: pull the loop lying on top, turn a twist over, pick a knot open, rub the dirt off, splice a tear with sticky tape. Wind the tape in with the pencil, put the cassette in the deck, press Play, and the music comes through the tape hiss while the needles dance.

  • 300 levels on five shelves by year: '91, '93, '95, '98 and 2000.
  • A cassette collection: clear, chrome, neon, golden, microcassettes, each with its own handwritten insert.
  • Original music in an 80s and 90s spirit: synthwave, eurodance, slow dances, 8-bit; play the tracks in full in My player.
  • Five tape players: a recorder with VU needles, a twin-deck boombox, a hi-fi deck, a car radio and a personal player.
  • Your own corner: wall carpet, posters, lamp, mat and desk to choose.
  • No timer, no rush: big brother's advice and undo are always at hand.
  • Play offline, in English and Russian.
