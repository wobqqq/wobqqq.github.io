Weather Dress Up

Look out of the window: drizzle, a blizzard or a heatwave? Dress the hero for the weather and send them outside.

Every level is a small weather puzzle. The thermometer and the drop show whether there is enough warmth and rain protection, and an umbrella, a scarf or a sun hat decides the day. Guess wrong and the hero comes back frozen, soaked or overheated, ready to change.

  • 200 levels: 4 seasons and 20 cities, from −40 to +38, downpours, blizzards, fog, black ice and thunderstorms.
  • 76 items in 15 layers: from thermal underwear and felt boots to sandals and sunscreen.
  • Your own hero, Anya or Misha, and 9 neighbours of every age.
  • My wardrobe: 329 colours and patterns, a seasonal collection and a cosy hallway.
  • Daily puzzle, tasks, achievements and holidays.
  • A hint and an undo when you are stuck.
  • Works offline, in English and Russian.
