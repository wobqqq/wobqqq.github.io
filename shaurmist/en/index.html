Shawarma Street

"One shawarma, extra sauce, no onions!"
"Coming right up!"

Open a tiny shawarma stall by the metro and make the best wraps in town. Slice the meat from the spit, add the fillings, wrap it up and grill it to a perfect golden crunch — your customers are waiting!

  • Cook to order: "no onions", "double cheese", "extra spicy" — remember who likes what.
  • The perfect crunch: time the grill just right.
  • Funny customers: hungry students, night-shift cab drivers, rushed office workers, picky foodies and food bloggers.
  • Build a chain: grow from a stall to a café, hire cooks and open spots all over the city.
  • Earn offline: your shops keep cooking while you're away.
  • Garlic or hot? Pick your team — the debaters at your window will test you.
  • Play offline.

From one tiny stall to a shawarma empire!
