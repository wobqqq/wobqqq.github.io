Privacy policy

The game "Shawarma Street: Food Tycoon" (the game). Effective from 14 October 2026.

What the game stores

Shift progress, money and rating stars, your shawarma chain, cooks, upgrades, your chosen team, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own; we do not receive this data. To count your chain's income fairly while the game is closed, the game remembers when it was last open — also only on your device.

Ads

The game shows ads from Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex LLC). To show and count ads, the ad SDK may process the device's advertising ID, device and operating system details, the IP address and ad interaction data. Yandex processes this data under its own policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential. You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics and collects no personal data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect children's data. Ad videos are watched only when the player chooses to.

Contact

Questions about data: the address will be published before release.
