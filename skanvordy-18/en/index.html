An evening programme for those who get the joke

Scanwords and word searches with grown-up words and double-entendre clues. Your hostess for the evening: Madame Rosa.

Feasts and toasts, flirting and dates, bar and cocktails, in-laws and office parties, casino and nightlife: solve with a smile. No swearing and nothing crude: all the spice is in the wording. The arrow scanwords are in Russian; word searches come in Russian and English.

  • Two modes in one game: 300 arrow scanwords and 300 word searches (plus 150 in English).
  • 15 cheeky themes: feasts, flirting, bar, marriage and in-laws, jokes, resort, passion, casino, money, office, fashion, single life, nightlife, jealousy, slang.
  • Witty clues: every word has three, from plain to sly with a wink.
  • Act of the day and word of the day: the same act for everyone, missed ones from the past week wait in the archive.
  • Three tasks a day, a weekly goal and gifts on the house.
  • Festive evenings for New Year, Valentine's Day, 23 February, 8 March and the velvet season, with keepsake brooches.
  • Personal dictionary: every word you solve with a short note, search and favourites.
  • Handy hints: open a letter or a word, check yourself, take an easier clue.
  • Play offline. No purchases.

Take a seat, the night is young.
