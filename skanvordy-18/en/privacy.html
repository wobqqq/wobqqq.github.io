Privacy policy

The game Scanwords for Adults (the game). Last updated: 27 October 2026.

What data

The game does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts or other personal data; it has no accounts and no in-app purchases; ads are non-personalised only (see "Ads"). The game itself does not need the internet: without it, it works as usual. When the internet is on, only the third-party SDKs built into the game go online, and only with what is described below: in both versions, Yandex and VK ads (see "Ads"); in the RuStore version, the RuStore rating SDK (see "Rating the game"). The game sends no statistics and no crash reports.

What stays on your device

Progress, solved puzzles, your personal dictionary, chips, cherries and settings are stored only on your device so you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

Reminders

Only if you agree, the game asks the phone for permission to show notifications and sets a local reminder: only after 7 days without opening the game, at most 3 times (after 7, 14 and 21 days), never at night. The phone shows it on schedule by itself, without the internet or any server; no data is sent anywhere. The permission to run after a restart is used only to set the reminder again. You can turn it off in the game's or the phone's settings at any time.

How long and how to delete

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Scanwords for Adults → Storage → Clear data).

Sharing

"Share" draws a card on your device with tonight's words, your streak, the game's name and its store link, and opens the system share window; you choose where and to whom to send it. The picture holds no personal data, and the game sends nothing by itself.

Rating the game

In the RuStore version, the "Rate the game" button and an occasional offer to rate the game open RuStore's official rating dialog. It is shown by the RuStore rating SDK (VK LLC) built into the game; if the dialog is unavailable, the game's RuStore page opens instead. You leave your rating and review in the RuStore app under your RuStore account, and RuStore handles them under its own terms: https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users.

Each time the RuStore version starts, and if the rating dialog fails to open, the SDK sends technical metrics to a RuStore server (stats-dg.rustore.ru): the game's package name and version, the SDK's name and version, the event type and its time, error details if something failed, and a random installation ID that the SDK creates on your device. This ID is not linked to your phone model, your account or your advertising ID, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address. Without internet, the metrics wait on the device and are sent later by a daily background task, including after the phone restarts. That is why the RuStore version needs the permissions to access the internet, check the network state and run after a restart. These metrics contain no name, phone number, email, advertising ID, phone model, location or game progress. RuStore keeps them as its terms say.

The AppGallery version has no RuStore dialog: "Rate the game" opens the game's AppGallery page. The game does not receive your data from the store.

Ads

The game shows ads through the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, YANDEX LLC), which fills some of them with VK Ads (VK LLC). Ads are non-personalised only: the game puts the SDK into its restricted-ads mode without interest targeting and does not pass on the advertising ID. To show and count ads, the SDK may receive the device model, Android version, language, IP address and ad impression and click data; the ad networks process this data under their policies: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/, https://help.mail.ru/legal/terms/adsvk/privacy/. The game itself does not store this data. The networks keep it as their policies say. Without internet no ads are shown and the game works as usual.

Writing to the developer

The "Write to the developer" button opens your mail app with a prepared letter (game version, Android version, phone model); you send the email yourself, nothing is sent automatically. Your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above); this also removes the SDKs' installation IDs. To stop the game from sending anything (ad requests, rating SDK metrics), block its internet access in the phone settings if your phone allows it (Settings → Apps → Scanwords for Adults → Data usage): the game works fully without the internet. For data Yandex or VK received, contact them under their rules (links above). You can also write to us with any question about data or to have your emails deleted.

Age 18+

The game is for adults only (18+): it has playful clues about parties, flirting, relationships, gambling and nightlife, with no obscene language and no explicit material. It is not meant for children and does not collect children's data. The game does not ask your age and does not receive your name, email, phone number, location or advertising ID.
