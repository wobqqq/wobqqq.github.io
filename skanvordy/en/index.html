Cozy Scanwords and Word Search

The daily paper for word lovers

Scanwords and word searches with a cozy paper look, always in your pocket.

Solve arrow scanwords, find hidden words in word searches and build a personal dictionary of every word you solve.

  • Two modes in one game: 300 scanwords with clues inside the grid and 450 word searches.
  • 15 themes: country house, kitchen, animals, fairy tales, cities, space, holidays and more.
  • Word of the day and a daily issue: the issue is the same for everyone; missed ones of the past week wait in the archive.
  • Three tasks every day and a goal of the week with a parcel.
  • Festive issues for New Year, spring and autumn, with keepsake stamps for your binder.
  • Your personal dictionary: every word you solve with a short note, search and favourites.
  • Handy hints: reveal a letter or a word, check yourself, or take an easier clue.
  • A five-puzzle tutorial — or skip it with "I know how".
  • Large letters, a timed mode and Grandma Tonya by your side.
  • Play offline. No ads, no purchases.

Please note: scanwords are in Russian; word searches are in English (150) and Russian (300).
