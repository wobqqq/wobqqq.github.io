Privacy policy

The game "Scanwords & Word Search" (the game). Last updated: 16 October 2026.

What the game stores

The game does not collect or transmit personal data. Progress, solved issues, your personal dictionary, clips, quills and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own.

Ads

The current version of the game has no ads. If ads are added in an update, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases.

Rating the game

The rating dialog is shown by the RuStore app under its own rules; the game receives no data from RuStore.

Writing to the developer

"Write to the developer" opens your mail app with a ready letter (game version, Android version, phone model). You see the letter before sending; nothing is sent automatically.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages (0+) and collects no data from children.
