Privacy policy

The game "Cozy Scanwords and Word Search" (the game). Last updated: 17 October 2026.

What data

The game does not collect personal data, store it on servers or share it with third parties. It has no accounts, ads, analytics of its own or in-app purchases, and it does not need the internet. The only part that goes online is the built-in RuStore rating SDK in the RuStore version (see "Rating the game").

What stays on your device

Your progress, solved issues, personal dictionary, clips, quills and settings are stored only on your device so you can continue playing. The developer has no access to them.

How long and how to delete

The data is kept while the game is installed. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in the phone settings (Settings → Apps → Cozy Scanwords and Word Search → Storage → Clear data).

Sharing

The "Share" button draws a newspaper clipping on the device with the day's words, your streak, the game's name and its store link, and opens the system share sheet; you choose where and to whom to send it. The picture has no personal data, and the game itself sends nothing.

Rating the game

In the RuStore version, the "Rate the game" button and an occasional offer to rate the game open RuStore's official rating dialog. It is shown by the RuStore rating SDK (VK LLC) built into the game; if the dialog is unavailable, the game's RuStore page opens instead. You leave your rating and review in the RuStore app under your RuStore account, and RuStore handles them under its own terms: https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users.

To run the rating dialog and keep track of how it works, the SDK sends technical metrics to a RuStore server: the game's package name and version, the SDK's name and version, the event type (for example, that the dialog was requested or shown) and its time. While there is no internet, the metrics wait on the device and are sent later, including after the phone restarts. That is why the game needs the permissions to access the internet, check the network state and run after a restart. These metrics contain no name, phone number, email, advertising ID, location or game progress. RuStore keeps them as its terms say.

The AppGallery version has no RuStore dialog: "Rate the game" opens the game's AppGallery page. The game does not receive your data from the store.

Writing to the developer

The "Write to the developer" button opens your mail app with a prepared letter (game version, Android version, phone model); you send the email yourself, nothing is sent automatically. Your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can delete the game's data at any time (see above) and write to us with any question about data or to have your emails deleted.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect children's data.
