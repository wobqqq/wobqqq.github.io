Steel Front

October 1941. The enemy is at the gates of Moscow, and whether the front holds depends on your orders.

Steel Front is a turn-based strategy game about the Second World War. On the campaign map you choose which region to win back next, and every battle is a tactical fight on hexes: riflemen in trenches, SMG squads, machine guns, anti-tank guns and T-34s. Cover saves lives, a flank shot gets through armour, a machine gun pins the enemy down, and the fog of war hides ambushes — for the enemy too.

  • The Battle of Moscow campaign: 10 regions from Kryukovo to Mozhaisk, enemy counter-attacks and defence battles, front-line briefings about real events.
  • Hex tactics: terrain and trenches, return fire, Overwatch, morale, fog of war and a loss forecast before every attack.
  • HQ before each battle: pick your units, refill their losses, buy new ones, call in an artillery strike.
  • Veterans: units go from battle to battle, earn stars and ranks, and you know their leaders by name.
  • Support and missions on the field: artillery strikes and smoke, a radio, a depot and a downed pilot.
  • A fair enemy: the AI sees only what its soldiers see; three difficulty levels.
  • Left in the middle of a battle? You come back to the same turn.
  • Daily operation and daily tasks; play offline, in English and Russian.
  • Coming soon: the USA and Britain campaigns.

No blood and no Nazi symbols: just the history of the front. Optionally, one reminder if you are away for a week; it can be turned off in the settings. The front is waiting, commander!
