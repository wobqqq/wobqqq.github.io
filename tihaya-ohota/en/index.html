Mushroom Picker

The quiet hunt begins: a warm forest, sunbeams through the crowns and the juicy "pop" of a porcini landing in your wicker basket.

Each glade grows real mushrooms of Russian and Belarusian forests. Pick everything the recipe asks for and leave the poisonous look-alikes alone: the false chanterelle, the bitter bolete, the death cap. Part the grass and sweep the fallen leaves: mushrooms love to hide.

  • 300 levels and a glade of the day: birch grove, pine forest, spruce wood, mossy brook, oak grove.
  • 27 real mushroom species, from porcini and saffron milk cap to fly agaric and false morel.
  • The mushroom guide: where each one grows, how to tell it, how it is cooked, what it looks like.
  • A magnifier and a second chance when in doubt; no timers.
  • Forest sounds: tits, a cuckoo, wind in the pines.
  • Play offline, in Russian and English.
