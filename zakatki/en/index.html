Pickle Sort

End of summer at the cottage — and everything's mixed up!

Pickles, tomatoes, peppers and jam ended up in the same jars. Move, sort and match until every jar holds one thing, then seal it with a satisfying click.

  • Simple rules, clever levels: easy to start, hard to put down.
  • All kinds of preserves: pickles, tomatoes, mushrooms, cherry and raspberry jam.
  • 1000 levels and more: spring, summer, autumn and the winter cellar.
  • Your own cellar: watch the shelves fill with hundreds of jars.
  • A dream cottage: a greenhouse, a gazebo, a samovar and a cat on the porch.
  • Pure relaxation: glass clinks and lid clicks, a "sounds only" mode.
  • Plays offline, in Russian and English.
