Privacy policy

The game "Pickle Sort" (the game). Effective from 16 October 2026.

What the game stores

Progress, stars, coins, the cellar, recipes, tasks and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts and no server of its own, and we do not receive this data.

Advertising

The current version of the game shows no ads. If ads are added in an update, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Analytics

The game uses no third-party analytics.

Purchases

The game has no purchases for real money.

Rating the game

The in-game rating window is shown by the RuStore app under its own rules; the game receives no data about you from RuStore.

Writing to the developer

"Write to the developer" opens your mail app with a prepared letter (game version, Android version, phone model). You see the letter before sending; nothing is sent automatically.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children.
